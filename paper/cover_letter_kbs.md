# Cover Letter for Knowledge-Based Systems

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled:

FRWKV+: Adaptive Periodic-Position Branch Interaction for Frequency-Space Linear Time Series Forecasting

for publication in *Knowledge-Based Systems*.

This work studies when periodic-position modulation is useful in frequency-space linear forecasting. Starting from an FRWKV backbone, we investigate cross-branch frequency interaction and adaptive periodic-position correction. The final manuscript is supported by:

- seven-dataset benchmarking on ETTh1, ETTh2, ETTm1, ETTm2, Weather, Exchange, and ILI
- selected-run provenance for every reported main-table cell
- matched 16-seed FRWKV-family ablations across 28 dataset-horizon settings
- runtime/profile evidence for the current main model recipes
- regime analysis covering both local gains and boundary cases

The main practical conclusion is that periodic-position information is most useful as a trust-controlled correction mechanism with dataset-dependent gains. We believe this fits the scope of *Knowledge-Based Systems* because the paper centers on an interpretable, knowledge-guided modulation mechanism, systematic evidence across multiple regimes, and practical decision rules for when the periodic-position correction should be used conservatively.

This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and agreed to its submission. Relevant declarations regarding funding, competing interests, data availability, and generative AI use are prepared separately for submission.

We appreciate your consideration.
